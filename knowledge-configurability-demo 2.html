Objective Trade-offs
L1 Decision Physics ADVISOR JUDGE STORYTELLER profitability concentration
Purpose
Encodes the fundamental tension between competing business objectives. Every decision involves trade-offs — pursuing one objective comes at the expense of another. BDI must always surface what is gained and what is given up.
Core Rules
// Must NEVER do: - Recommend maximizing one objective without acknowledging what is sacrificed - Present any decision as costless - Ignore capital constraints when discussing growth opportunities // Must remain flexible: - Relative weighting of objectives - Which trade-offs are most material
Good vs Bad Example
Good: "Pursuing multifamily growth deploys $500M capital, improving NII by 12% but reducing RORAC from 14.2% to 12.8%. Trade-off: higher income now, less capital flexibility later."
Bad: "We should grow the multifamily portfolio because it has good returns."

Define Rules & Guardrails
What must BDI never do when applying this skill? What should remain flexible? What must not be hardcoded? These guardrails protect reasoning integrity while allowing client-specific configuration.
Must NEVER do
Recommend holding a position without disclosing liquidity risk and time-to-exit cost. Present mark-to-market as realized value. Assume forced-sale proceeds equal fair value.
Should remain flexible
Liquidity discount rate (client-specific). Exit timeline assumptions (varies by asset class). Market stress severity for haircut scenarios.

Client Engagement
How we configure BDI with clients
A structured engagement workflow ensures knowledge is tailored to each institution's decision culture.
1
Discovery & Mapping
Identify the client's decision workflows, stakeholders, risk appetite, and governance rules. Map their existing processes to BDI's knowledge layer model.
2
Knowledge Seed & Calibration
Deploy base skills and calibrate L1 decision physics to the client's objectives, hurdle rates, and capital model. Configure L3 bindings for their specific governance requirements.
3
Prompt Testing & Validation
Run the client's real questions through the configurator. Verify skill matching, coverage gaps, and output quality. Iterate until the knowledge layer matches their reasoning expectations.
4
Collaborative Refinement
Client stakeholders use the configurator to vote, comment, request edits, and propose improvements. Knowledge evolves through structured feedback loops.
5
Continuous Knowledge Evolution
Coverage dashboard monitors health. New skills added through guided wizards. Gap analysis triggers proactive improvements. Knowledge scales with the client's needs.
